\section{Grade 10: Continuous Probability Distributions}\label{sec:grade10-term3-overview}

Complex and non-standard problems solving.

Evaluate finance context situations with normal distribution and other continuous random variables.

\begin{itemize}
	\item Area of simple geometric figures.
	\item Random variable.
	\item Continuous probability distribution.
	\item Mean and variance.
	\item Normal distribution.
	\item Exponential distribution.
	\item t-student distribution.
	\item Investment portfolios.
	\item Risk.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assessment Criteria}\label{sec:grade10-term3-criteria}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Describes probabilities in continuous variables as a density function.
	\item Explains how a uniform random variable works.
	\item Expresses how to work with distributions that have a linear behaviour.
	\item Interprets the concept of variance and standard deviation of a probability function.
	\item Uses the normal standard distribution and its tables to calculate probabilities in context situations.
	\item Employs the standardisation of the normal variable in real life problems.
	\item Solves problems through the concept of inverse normal distribution.
	\item Summarises the elements and characteristics that an investment portfolio may have.
	\item Establishes that the risk of a portfolio is determined by its probability distribution.
	\item Evaluates finance context situations with normal distribution and other continuous random variables.
\end{enumerate}
