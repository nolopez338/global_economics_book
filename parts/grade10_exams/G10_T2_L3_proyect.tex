	\ExamTitleBlock{10th grade}{Term 2 -- Lesson 3 PROYECT: JavaScript Decision Analysis Project (C10 evidence)}{}
	
	\ExamSection{Project brief}
	\subsection*{Introduction}
	This programming project asks students to implement a JavaScript tool that automates C8 decision-analysis outputs (Bayes/EV, Maximin, Minimax Regret) and then uses those outputs to support a C9 decision-tree conclusion.
	Presenting and defending this program is the evidence for Criterion 10.

	\subsection*{Input specification}
	The program receives one payoff table with:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item States of nature and their probabilities.
		\item Alternatives.
		\item Payoff value for every alternative-state pair.
	\end{itemize}
	Example input format (values in thousand USD):
	\begin{center}
		\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
			\toprule
			State of nature & Probability & $A_1$ & $A_2$ & $A_3$ \\
			\midrule
			$S_1$ & 0.40 & 140 & 130 & 155 \\
			$S_2$ & 0.35 & 95 & 105 & 90 \\
			$S_3$ & 0.25 & 60 & 80 & 45 \\
			\bottomrule
		\end{tabular}
	\end{center}

	\subsection*{Output specification}
	For each alternative, the program must return:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item maximum payoff,
		\item minimum payoff,
		\item expected value (using provided probabilities),
		\item maximum regret.
	\end{itemize}
	The output format is free, but it must be consistent and readable.
	A recommended summary table is:
	\begin{center}
		\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
			\toprule
			Alternative & Max payoff & Min payoff & EV & Max regret \\
			\bottomrule
		\end{tabular}
	\end{center}

	\subsection*{Workflow requirement (C8 to C9)}
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item Run the JavaScript program and generate tables.
		\item Use those tables to conclude C8 rankings (Bayes, Maximin, Minimax Regret).
		\item Use the C8 recommendation as input for a C9 decision tree with second-stage uncertainty.
	\end{enumerate}
	After presenting the program, the student may proceed to solve the project problems in \texttt{G10\_T2\_L3\_proyect\_problems.tex}.

	\subsection*{Program-produced regret table}
	\begin{center}

			\toprule
			Alternative & $H$ regret & $M$ regret & $L$ regret & Max regret \\
			\midrule
			$A_1$ & 15 & 14 & 26 & 26 \\
			$A_2$ & 23 & 0 & 0 & 23 \\
			$A_3$ & 0 & 22 & 43 & 43 \\

	\subsection*{Program-produced C8 summary table}
	\begin{center}
		\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
			\toprule
			Alternative & Max payoff & Min payoff & EV & Max regret \\
			\midrule
			$A_1$ & 150 & 52 & 112.20 & 26 \\

			$A_3$ & 165 & 35 & 112.75 & 43 \\
			\bottomrule
		\end{tabular}
	\end{center}

	\subsection*{How to conclude C8 using program outputs}
	From the summary table:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Bayes (EV) selects $A_2$.
		\item Maximin (largest minimum) selects $A_2$.
		\item Minimax Regret (smallest max regret) selects $A_2$.
	\end{itemize}
	So the C8 recommendation is $A_2$.

	\subsection*{How to conclude C9 using C8 result}
	Second-stage uncertainty for the tree:
	stable regulation (0.60, adjustment $+12$) and tightened regulation (0.40, adjustment $-18$).
	Program-assisted branch table:
	\begin{center}
		\begin{tabular}{l c c}
			\toprule
			Branch & Probability & Adjusted payoff from $A_2$ \\
			\midrule
			Stable regulation & 0.60 & 130.70 \\
			Tightened regulation & 0.40 & 100.70 \\
			\bottomrule
		\end{tabular}
	\end{center}
	The decision tree therefore confirms $A_2$ as final recommendation.

	The decision tree concludes with $D_4$ as final recommendation under the two-stage process.
