		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{1. Project brief}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		\textbf{Introduction}\par
		This programming project asks students to implement a JavaScript tool that automates C8 decision-analysis outputs (Bayes/EV, Maximin, Minimax Regret) and then uses those outputs to support a C9 decision-tree conclusion.
		Presenting and defending this program is the evidence for Criterion 10.
		
		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\textbf{Input specification}\par
		The program receives one payoff table with:
		\begin{itemize}
			\item States of nature and their probabilities.
			\item Alternatives.
			\item Payoff value for every alternative-state pair.
		\end{itemize}
		Example input format (values in thousand USD):
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
				\hline
				State of nature & Probability & $A_1$ & $A_2$ & $A_3$ \\
				\hline
				$S_1$ & 0.40 & 140 & 130 & 155 \\
				$S_2$ & 0.35 & 95 & 105 & 90 \\
				$S_3$ & 0.25 & 60 & 80 & 45 \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\textbf{Output specification}\par
		For each alternative, the program must return:
		\begin{itemize}
			\item maximum payoff,
			\item minimum payoff,
			\item expected value (using provided probabilities),
			\item maximum regret.
		\end{itemize}
		The output format is free, but it must be consistent and readable.
		A recommended summary table is:
		\hspace{-5mm}
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
				\hline
				Alt. & Max payoff & Min payoff & EV & Max regret \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\textbf{Workflow requirement (C8 to C9)}\par
		\begin{enumerate}
			\item Run the JavaScript program and generate tables.
			\item Use those tables to conclude C8 rankings (Bayes, Maximin, Minimax Regret).
			\item Use the C8 recommendation as input for a C9 decision tree with second-stage uncertainty.
		\end{enumerate}
		After presenting the program, the student may proceed to solve the project problems in \texttt{G10\_T2\_L3\_proyect\allowbreak\_problems.tex}.
		
		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{2. Solved example 1 (program-output guided)}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		\textbf{Base payoff table}\par
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
				\hline
				State & Probability & $A_1$ & $A_2$ & $A_3$ \\
				\hline
				$H$ & 0.45 & 150 & 142 & 165 \\
				$M$ & 0.35 & 98 & 112 & 90 \\
				$L$ & 0.20 & 52 & 78 & 35 \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\textbf{Program-produced regret table}\par
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
				\hline
				Alt. & $H$ regret & $M$ regret & $L$ regret & Max regret \\
				\hline
				$A_1$ & 15 & 14 & 26 & 26 \\
				$A_2$ & 23 & 0 & 0 & 23 \\
				$A_3$ & 0 & 22 & 43 & 43 \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\textbf{Program-produced C8 summary table}\par
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
				\hline
				Alt. & Max P. & Min P. & EV & Max regret \\
				\hline
				$A_1$ & 150 & 52 & 112.20 & 26 \\
				$A_2$ & 142 & 78 & 118.70 & 23 \\
				$A_3$ & 165 & 35 & 112.75 & 43 \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		
		\textbf{How to conclude C8 using program outputs}\par
		From the summary table:
		\begin{itemize}
			\item Bayes (EV) selects $A_2$.
			\item Maximin (largest minimum) selects $A_2$.
			\item Minimax Regret (smallest max regret) selects $A_2$.
		\end{itemize}
		So the C8 recommendation is $A_2$.
		
		\textbf{How to conclude C9 using C8 result}\par
		Second-stage uncertainty for the tree:
		stable regulation (0.60, adjustment $+12$) and tightened regulation (0.40, adjustment $-18$).

		Step 1. Reconfirm branch outcomes for the C8-selected alternative.
		\[
		\text{Stable branch}=118.70+12=130.70,
		\qquad
		\text{Tightened branch}=118.70-18=100.70.
		\]

		Step 3. Final decision.
		\begin{center}
			\begin{tabular}{l}
				\hline
				Decision tree summary \\
				\hline
				$D_0$: choose C8-recommended strategy $A_2$ \\
				$\rightarrow C_1$: regulation stable (0.60) $\to 130.70$ \\
				$\rightarrow C_2$: regulation tightens (0.40) $\to 100.70$ \\
				Equivalent value at the chance node: $EV_{tree}(A_2)=0.60(130.70)+0.40(100.70)=118.70$ \\
				Root equivalent value: $118.70$ \\
				Final leaf selected: $A_2$ \\
				\hline
			\end{tabular}
		\end{center}
		The decision tree therefore confirms $A_2$ as final recommendation.
