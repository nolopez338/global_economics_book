\section{Grade 11: Continuous Probability Distributions}\label{sec:grade11-term1-overview}

\subsection{Learning Strand}\label{sec:grade11-term1-strand}

Complex and non-standard problems solving.

\subsection{Learning Objective}\label{sec:grade11-term1-objective}

Evaluate finance context situations with normal distribution and other continuous random variables.

\subsection{Assessment Criteria}\label{sec:grade11-term1-criteria}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Describes continuous random variables considering the concept.
	\item Interprets probabilities in continuous variables as the area under the curve.
	\item Explains how a uniform random variable works.
	\item Shows how to work with distributions that have other shapes, like triangular or circular.
	\item Uses the normal distribution and its tables to calculate probabilities in context situations.
	\item Employs the standardization of the normal variable in complex problems.
	\item Solves problems through the concept of inverse normal distribution.
	\item Differentiates the elements and characteristics that an investment portfolio may have.
	\item Establishes the risk of a portfolio based on its probability distribution.
	\item Evaluates finance context situations with normal distribution and other continuous random variables.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Topics}\label{sec:grade11-term1-topics}

\begin{itemize}
	\item Area of simple geometric figures.
	\item Random variable.
	\item Continuous probability distribution.
	\item Mean and variance.
	\item Normal distribution.
	\item Exponential distribution.
	\item t-student distribution.
	\item Investment portfolios.
	\item Risk.
\end{itemize}
