\chapter{Grade 11: Hypothesis Tests}\label{sec:grade11-term3-overview}

\section{Learning Strand}\label{sec:grade11-term3-strand}

Complex and non-standard problems solving.

\section{Learning Objective}\label{sec:grade11-term3-objective}

Model a research question in which a statistical hypothesis must be tested.

\section{Assessment Criteria}\label{sec:grade11-term3-criteria}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Describes the concept of statistical hypothesis.
	\item Identifies the null and alternative hypothesis in a statistical test.
	\item Determines whether a test should be one tailed or two tailed.
	\item Applies the general procedure for testing a hypothesis with fixed probability in context situations.
	\item Calculates the hypothesis tests on the mean of a population.
	\item Establishes the right hypothesis on a test about the proportion of a population.
	\item Contrasts the P-values in hypotheses tests with the reference probability.
	\item Analyses the relationship between hypothesis testing and confidence interval estimation.
	\item Evaluates context situations in finance and economy in which a statistical hypothesis appears.
	\item Models a research question in which a statistical hypothesis must be tested.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Key Topics}\label{sec:grade11-term3-topics}

\begin{itemize}
	\item Area of simple geometric figures.
	\item Sample and population.
	\item Random variable.
	\item Continuous probability distribution.
	\item Mean and variance.
	\item Normal distribution.
	\item t-student distribution.
	\item Investment portfolios.
	\item Optimization problems with maxima and minima.
\end{itemize}
