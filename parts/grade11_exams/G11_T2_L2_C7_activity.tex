		\SubsectionBox{Criteria assessment}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		Each assessment criterion is evaluated across the three problems. A criterion is considered passed when it is correctly activated in at least two of the three problems.
		
		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{1. Community Water Pricing Support}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A city council surveys \textbf{220 households} about support for a new water pricing plan. In the sample, \textbf{143 households} say they support the plan.
		
		Construct and interpret a \textbf{95\% confidence interval} for the population proportion of households that support the plan.

		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{2. Coastal Ferry Reliability}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A transport board reviews \textbf{180 ferry trips} and finds that \textbf{45} arrived at least fifteen minutes late. The board wants a proportion estimate for the late arrival rate.
		
		Construct and interpret a \textbf{90\% confidence interval} for the population proportion of late ferry trips.

		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{3. Small Business Grant Renewals}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A development agency tracks \textbf{360 grants} issued to small businesses. During the renewal period, \textbf{54 grants} were not renewed.
		
		Construct and interpret \textbf{90\% and 95\% confidence intervals} for the population proportion of grants that are not renewed.

		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{4. Household Digital Banking Use}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A financial inclusion survey studies \textbf{160 households} and finds that \textbf{104 households} use digital banking services.
		
		Construct and interpret \textbf{90\%, 95\%, and 99\% confidence intervals} for the population proportion of households that use digital banking services.

		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{5. Port Inspection Compliance}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A port authority evaluates compliance with a new inspection checklist. Sample A includes \textbf{250 inspections} with \textbf{170} compliant. Sample B includes \textbf{180 inspections} with \textbf{110} compliant. Assume both samples are drawn from the same population of inspections.
		
		Construct and interpret a \textbf{95\% confidence interval} for the population proportion of compliant inspections for each sample.

		\vspace{0.25cm}
		\SubsectionBox{6. Public Market Vendor Licensing}\vspace{-0.25cm}
		A city reviews vendor licensing in two districts. Sample A includes \textbf{300 vendors} with \textbf{96} licensed. Sample B includes \textbf{210 vendors} with \textbf{60} licensed.
		
		Construct and interpret \textbf{90\% and 98\% confidence intervals} for the population proportion of licensed vendors for each sample.
